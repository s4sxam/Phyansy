Phyansy — Free Physics Reference for Students

Phyansy is a free interactive physics reference tool for high-school and university students. Browse 295 physics equations with full derivations, 79 physical constants with CODATA-standard values, 51 Greek and mathematical symbols, SI base units, derived units, metric prefixes, and a physics equation calculator. Available in 14 languages including Hindi, Bengali, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi, Arabic, Japanese, German, Russian, French, Spanish, Portuguese, and Chinese. No sign-up required.

Topics covered include Classical Mechanics, Electromagnetism, Thermodynamics, Quantum Mechanics, Special Relativity, Optics, Wave Physics, Fluid Mechanics, Nuclear Physics, and Astrophysics. Key equations include Newton's laws, Maxwell's equations, Schrödinger equation, Planck's law, Einstein's mass-energy equivalence, and hundreds more.

About Phyansy

Phyansy is a free interactive physics reference built by Tanay, a developer passionate about making physics accessible to students worldwide. The project covers 295 physics equations with full derivations and context, 79 physical constants with CODATA-standard values, 51 symbols, SI units, and a built-in calculator — all free, with no sign-up required.

Constant values are sourced from CODATA 2018 recommended values. The project is open-source on GitHub. Third-party services: Google Translate (for 14-language support) and KaTeX (for math rendering). See our Privacy policy for details.
